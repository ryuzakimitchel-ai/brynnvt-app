Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026
Brynn Interactive is designed primarily as a local desktop application. This policy explains what information the app may process when you connect third-party services such as Twitch, TikTok, OBS Studio or local bridge software.

1. Information Brynn Interactive may process

Depending on the features you enable, the application may process:

  • Account identifiers and public profile information returned by connected services.
  • OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens required to maintain authorized connections.
  • Chat messages, usernames, platform identifiers, emotes, gifts, follows, subscriptions, likes, shares and other supported stream events.
  • OBS configuration data such as scenes, sources, filters and connection settings.
  • Local application settings, emote libraries, commands, automations, goals, rankings and reward configuration.

2. How information is used

Information is used only to provide the features you request, including displaying unified chat, sending authorized chat messages, triggering automations, controlling OBS, operating overlays, applying moderation actions and maintaining local application preferences.

3. Local storage

Brynn Interactive stores most configuration data locally on the device running the application. Authentication credentials may be stored in the application's local data directory so that you do not need to reconnect every time the application starts.

4. Third-party services

When you connect a third-party platform, that platform processes data under its own privacy policy. Brynn Interactive only requests permissions required for enabled functionality. You can revoke platform authorization through the relevant platform's account or developer settings.

5. Data sharing and sale

Brynn Interactive does not sell personal information. The application does not intentionally share personal information with advertisers or unrelated third parties. Data may be transmitted to a connected platform only when necessary to perform an action you requested, such as sending a chat message or performing a moderation operation.

6. Chat and live-stream data

Chat and stream events may be displayed in Brynn Interactive, in local overlays, and in OBS browser sources configured by you. If you choose to broadcast those overlays, that information may become visible to viewers of your stream.

7. Retention

Local data remains on your device until you delete it, reset the application, uninstall it and remove its local data, or otherwise clear the relevant configuration. Third-party platforms may retain data according to their own policies.

8. Security

Reasonable measures are used to avoid exposing authentication secrets through web-facing interfaces. However, no software system can guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for protecting access to the computer on which Brynn Interactive is installed.

9. Children's privacy

Brynn Interactive is intended for streamers and creators who are legally able to operate and connect the relevant third-party accounts. The application is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Brynn Interactive adds or changes integrations. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.

11. Contact

Privacy questions can be directed through the official support or project contact channel distributed with Brynn Interactive. If you publish a dedicated support email or website contact form, you should replace this paragraph with that direct contact method.

12. Platform independence

Brynn Interactive is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Twitch, TikTok, OBS Studio, Amazon, ByteDance, or their affiliates.
