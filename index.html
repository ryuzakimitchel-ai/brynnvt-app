Desktop streaming toolkit

Bring your stream tools into one control center.

Brynn Interactive is an independent desktop application for streamers. It combines unified chat, OBS control, automation, alerts, emotes, commands, goals and community rewards in a single local-first workspace.

Emotes & commands

Build visual sequences with audio timelines, animations, clips and reusable emote assets.

Community rewards

Assign temporary weekly privileges, emotes and commands to ranking winners.
